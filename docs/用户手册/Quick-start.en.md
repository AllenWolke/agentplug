# Quick start

[All guides](README.en.md) · [中文版](快速开始.md)

1. Sign in and check the company workspace and department on the left. Your account belongs to one company and may join multiple departments; agent and data access are granted separately.
2. On the workspace home page, choose an available assistant by its purpose. The examples cover operations diagnosis, document questions and ticket reply drafts.
3. Select **Use example**, or fill in the business form. Required fields must be completed. **Advanced input** accepts the agent's JSON contract.
4. Select **Start task** once. Watch its status while it runs. If the live connection drops, check the original run's status before considering another submission.
5. Read the result and verify its sources and limitations. Expand the full response and run ID when you need technical details.
6. Open **My run history** to retrieve saved input and answers. History remains subject to your current access.

Examples produce answers or drafts. They do not automatically execute code, send a ticket response or enable agent collaboration. Additional actions require their own configuration and approval.

If an assistant requires access, open it, explain your purpose and submit a request. Track it in your access requests and refresh access after approval. Signing in or submitting a request does not itself grant resource access.

Language switching preserves your draft and does not change an active task's input or output. To try an English example, select English before inserting a new example into an empty form.
